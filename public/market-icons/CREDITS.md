Country/category PNGs rendered from the existing NexaTrade reference SVG assets.

Additional cryptocurrency logos from CoinGecko coin metadata: https://www.coingecko.com/en/api . Company/index icons are the respective official website favicons, cached locally through Google favicon lookup. Instrument/country artwork is rendered locally.

Fantom, dYdX and Kaspa logos are cached from CoinGecko's `fantom`, `dydx` and `kaspa` coin metadata; Render's legacy RNDR ticker uses the bundled RENDER logo. Oil instruments use the locally drawn barrel/droplet SVG (no company logo applies to synthetic crude oil).
